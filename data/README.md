# SKYZR data (all synthetic except the county map)

Generated by a seeded script kept outside this repo. Do not hand-edit; ask Sai to regenerate.

| File | Rows | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `nc_counties.geojson` | 100 features | NC county outlines from Plotly's public county GeoJSON (github.com/plotly/datasets, `geojson-counties-fips.json`). Feature `id` is the 5-digit FIPS string. Real data. |
| `answer_key.csv` | 29 counties × hours 0–72 | Fictional ground truth: power % out, cell % out, the 5 lifeline colors, `overall_before_silence`, and `true_need_*` for the 9 resources. Drives answer-key mode and scoring. Never sent to the AI. |
| `feeds.csv` | every 3 h per hit county | Power % out and cell % out, whole numbers. Read by code, not the AI. Missing inside the 4 silent windows. At each feed hour, its colors match the answer key. |
| `reports.csv` | 50 | `report_id`, `sim_hour`, `county`, `fips`, `source` (role label), `verified` (yes/no), `text`. |
| `stock.csv` | 100 | On-hand stock of the 9 resources at hour 0, with `hit_or_grey`. Zero means zero. |

Read every CSV with `dtype=str` for `fips` so the leading digits survive.

## How reports are used
- **The AI is given** `sim_hour`, `source`, `verified` and `text` for one report at a time. It is not given `county` or `fips` and must return the county itself; those two columns exist so answer-key mode can run the silence clock without the AI, and so scoring can check the AI's county.
- **The AI extracts roads, water, health and medical, and resource requests only.** Power and communications come from `feeds.csv`. No report text makes a power or comms claim.
- Every report counts as contact for its county's silence clock; so does every feed reading (plan v2 Fix 1).

## Fixed vocabulary for AI output
Use exactly these strings, so AI output can be matched against the scoring key.

| kind | lifeline_or_resource | metric | status_word | value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| lifeline | Roads | Route status | Open, Partly closed, No route | Open / Some primary routes closed / No open route to county seat |
| lifeline | Water | Systems out of service | Out, or Normal when the count is 0 | the number of systems out (use this whenever a count is stated or clear, e.g. "the water system" = 1). **0 means no system is out: status_word "Normal", never "Out"**, e.g. "All three water systems are back in service" = Systems out of service, 0, Normal. A count of 0 can never make Water red or yellow by itself |
| lifeline | Water | System status | Normal, Boil notice, Out | Normal / Boil-water notice / System out of service (when no count is given) |
| lifeline | Health and Medical | Hospital status | Normal, On generator, Evacuating, Closed | Normal / Hospital on generator or diverting / Hospital closed or evacuating |
| resource | Water, Generators, Utility crews, Search-and-rescue teams, Food, Shelter, Medical teams, Hygiene items, Sanitation | Requested | Requested | the number requested |

Resource units: tankers, units, crews, teams, pallets of meals, cots and shelter kits, teams, kits, portable toilet and handwashing units.

## Thresholds (design doc)
- Power % customers out: green under 10; yellow 10 to under 50; red 50 or more.
- Communications % cell sites out: green under 10; yellow 10 to under 40; red 40 or more.
- Roads: open / some primary routes closed / no open route to the county seat.
- Water: normal / boil-water notice / a system out of service.
- Health and Medical: normal / a hospital on generator or diverting / a hospital closed or evacuating.
